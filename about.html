Founded in 2026, status co is a cultural studio working across collectibles and collaborations. Positioned between art, design, and everyday life, the studio produces, curates, and presents contemporary collectibles by artists and designers through curated pop-up drops. These objects are created with intention—meant to be lived with rather than stored, transforming functional forms into meaningful artifacts. Collaboration is also at the core of the studio's practice; status co closely connects creatives with brands, institutions, and corporations to develop projects that translate creative authorship into new contexts.

status co launched its first-ever drop, CONTEMPORARY ARTIFACTS, and its first collaboration, "what's your status?", at Art Show between February 13–15, 2026. Bringing together works by eleven artists and designers, the inaugural drop was built on the proposition that the functional objects shaping our daily lives deserve the same attention and authorship as traditional art. As part of this exhibition, the studio introduced a conceptual, participatory collectible: a container filled with sculpted paper forms functioning as contemporary fortunes. These artifacts were not purchased but encountered, offering an alternative form of collecting. For this first collaboration, a donation was made to the TEMA Foundation on behalf of Art Show x status co to give the objects an ecological presence alongside their conceptual meaning.
